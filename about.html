About Us

Empowering Kwadaso through responsible waste management

Who We Are

Kwadaso Waste Collection is a municipal initiative focused on providing efficient, safe, and eco-friendly waste management services to residents, businesses, and markets in the Kwadaso Municipality.

We’ve combined technology, local partnerships, and education to build a smarter and cleaner community. Our system allows households and businesses to schedule pickups, and make secure payments—all from one platform.

Our Mission

To promote public health and environmental sustainability by simplifying waste collection and making it accessible to all.

Our Vision

A cleaner, greener Kwadaso where every citizen plays a part in managing waste responsibly.
